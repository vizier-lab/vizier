Design notes

  • The new strip item only appears in project chats, after the changes count. Its number is the servers that are ready.
  • The dot shows the worst state: green pulsing while starting, amber for a missing server, red for a failed one.
  • Servers are grouped by language because an override applies to the whole language, across every root.
  • Only roots the agent has touched are listed. Nothing appears until a server is needed.
  • Component changes needed: StatusStripItemId gains an LSP item, and busy becomes a dot tone. The panel reuses StatusPanel and Switch.
  • Closes on ×, Escape or a press outside, per the dismissal convention.
