Design notes

  • The banner sits above the shell frame, so it shows on every page — Home, Settings and chat alike.
  • It says what is unavailable, not "you are offline": the title names the condition, the second line names what stops working and what keeps working.
  • It uses the offline tone of design/Banner: the retrying edge and ground, with a retrying dot.
  • The second line needs the offline tone to render its message. Today only the failed tone does, so the design component changes first.
  • "Try again" re-opens the socket. The chat's own banner keeps its per-topic line; this one is the shell-wide statement.
  • On a LAN address the browser refuses the worker, so a notice says so: the info tone of design/Notice, above the banner, in the same place.
